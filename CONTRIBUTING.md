# Contributing

Issues and pull requests are welcome.

Before submitting a change:

1. Keep the workflow limited to lawful, publicly accessible business information.
2. Do not add private customer records, credentials or guessed contact details.
3. Keep `SKILL.md` concise and move detailed methodology into `references/`.
4. Validate the Skill structure before committing.
5. Include a short explanation and an example for behavior changes.
