# Agarwood Prospect Search Playbook

Build multiple search routes instead of repeating one keyword.

## Route 1 — direct agarwood / oud
Combine target market with terms such as:
- agarwood distributor
- agarwood wholesaler
- agarwood importer
- oud wholesaler
- oudh distributor
- agarwood incense supplier
- agarwood retailer

## Route 2 — incense channel
- incense distributor
- incense wholesaler
- natural incense importer
- joss stick wholesaler
- incense brand wholesale
- incense store distributor
- incense burner wholesaler

## Route 3 — adjacent fragrance channel
- sandalwood distributor
- bakhoor wholesaler
- aromatherapy wholesaler
- essential oil distributor incense
- home fragrance distributor incense
- meditation products wholesaler
- spiritual goods distributor incense

## Route 4 — local language / market wording
When the target market is not primarily English-speaking, add obvious local-language equivalents for distributor, wholesale, incense, oud, agarwood, aromatherapy, spiritual goods and importer. Use public search only; do not bypass access controls.

## Route 5 — trade / exhibitor / directory evidence
Use reputable public trade-show exhibitor lists, chambers, distributor directories, industry associations, procurement portals and official business registries for discovery and identity confirmation.

## Search strategy
1. Start broad enough to build 3-5x the requested candidate count.
2. Prefer official company domains early.
3. Search within a confirmed domain for contact, about, wholesale, distributor, stockist, brands, incense, agarwood, oud, sandalwood, bakhoor, burner and aromatherapy pages.
4. If a candidate is strong but contact is missing, search public company-name + email/contact/WhatsApp terms.
5. Stop when the accepted count is reached or the raw-hit cap is reached.

Do not accept candidates merely because a search snippet contains the keywords. Verify business identity and relevance on accessible sources.
