# Agarwood Overseas B2B ICP

Use this reference to decide whether a company is genuinely worth developing for agarwood-related products.

## Product families

Primary product families:
- agarwood incense sticks / joss sticks / stick incense
- agarwood coil incense / spiral incense
- agarwood bracelets / prayer beads / wood bead accessories
- agarwood chips / chunks / shavings / raw aromatic material
- incense burners / electric burners / charcoal burners / coil holders
- agarwood gift sets and fragrance-related accessories

Adjacent product families that may indicate fit:
- sandalwood incense
- oud / oudh products
- bakhoor
- natural incense
- essential oils and aromatherapy
- meditation / yoga / spiritual fragrance products
- Buddhist / temple incense supplies
- home fragrance and ritual fragrance

## Priority customer types

Tier A — highest priority:
- importer
- distributor
- wholesaler
- established incense brand with multiple SKUs
- aromatherapy / natural fragrance brand with wholesale or dealer channels
- oud / agarwood specialist retailer with B2B or multi-store activity

Tier B — good priority:
- established incense retailer with a broad product range
- meditation / spiritual goods distributor
- temple / religious supply wholesaler
- home fragrance company that already sells natural incense or burners
- gift / lifestyle brand with incense or aromatherapy lines

Tier C — review manually:
- small single-store retailer
- marketplace-only seller
- general gift shop with only one incense SKU
- essential-oil seller with no incense, oud, sandalwood, ritual fragrance, or burner evidence

Reject or strongly deprioritize:
- news/blog/editorial sites
- schools/research institutes without commercial purchasing evidence
- pure forestry/agriculture pages with no product sales
- unrelated perfume/cosmetics companies with no oud/incense/aromatherapy relevance
- inactive or parked domains
- directories with no identifiable company behind the listing
- pure B2C marketplace storefronts when no independent business identity can be verified

## Fit scoring (0-100)

Score each accepted prospect using evidence, not assumptions.

Business relevance, 0-35:
- 30-35: directly sells agarwood/oud/incense or closely related products
- 20-29: sells sandalwood, bakhoor, natural fragrance, spiritual fragrance, or burners
- 10-19: adjacent aromatherapy/home-fragrance business with plausible cross-sell
- 0-9: weak adjacency

Channel value, 0-25:
- 22-25: importer/distributor/wholesaler/multi-location or explicit B2B
- 15-21: brand with dealer/wholesale capability
- 8-14: established retailer with broad range
- 0-7: small retail only

Contactability, 0-20:
- 18-20: official public sales/purchasing email or labeled WhatsApp
- 12-17: official general email or business phone
- 5-11: reputable third-party business contact only
- 0-4: weak or no contact evidence

Product-gap opportunity, 0-15:
- 12-15: clear gaps where our product families expand their assortment
- 7-11: partial overlap with meaningful complementary products
- 1-6: mostly overlap, limited differentiation
- 0: no credible product opportunity

Buying/expansion signal, 0-5:
- 4-5: wholesale inquiry, distributor recruitment, new category launch, trade-show activity, procurement signal
- 2-3: active catalog expansion or recent relevant content
- 0-1: no signal found

Qualification guidance:
- 80-100: priority_A
- 65-79: priority_B
- 50-64: review_needed
- below 50: skip unless user explicitly wants broader coverage

## Product-gap analysis

For every accepted company, identify 1-3 concrete opportunities, for example:
- sells incense but has few agarwood variants -> propose agarwood stick/coil incense
- sells bracelets but assortment is visually repetitive -> propose more varied agarwood bead combinations
- sells incense but burner range is narrow -> propose coil holders, chip burners, or electric burners
- sells oud/bakhoor but not East Asian agarwood incense -> propose complementary agarwood incense line
- sells meditation products -> propose compact incense + burner gift sets

Do not claim the company lacks a product unless the checked catalog/source reasonably supports that conclusion. Use wording such as "no such product was found in the reviewed catalog pages" when coverage is incomplete.
